How to play

  • Pan: drag the map. Pinch with two fingers (or scroll wheel) to zoom at any time.
  • Track: tap a start, then an end (towns snap). The cheapest route is suggested. Water needs your OK for a bridge.
  • Bridge: tap land, then land across 1 to 3 water tiles in a straight line.
  • Demolish: tap two tiles on a straight line to tear up track there for a partial refund.
  • Station: tap a tile with track and pick a size. Bigger stations reach more houses.
  • Train: tap a station to buy a train, then tap its stops in order and press Done. Tap a train with the Pan tool to see how it is doing.
  • Double: tap two tiles on existing track to add a second line between them, so trains can pass each other. Trains wait at stations, double-track tiles and junctions rather than entering single track with an opposing train on it.
  • Send to…: in the train list or on a train, send it to any station once. It then returns to its route.
  • Deposits and buildings: coal mines, timber camps and grain farms make raw goods. Open a station that serves a town and tap Build… to add a sawmill, mill, bakery or coal yard; the town buys what they make.
  • Locomotives: each model is on sale for a few decades and sets how fast a train runs and how many cars it pulls. Locomotives age: they cost more to run each year and break down more once worn out. At a station you can overhaul one (age back to 0, while its model is on sale) or replace it with a newer model.
  • Cars: each kind of goods needs its own car: coaches for passengers, mail & baggage cars for mail, coal hoppers for coal, flatcars for timber and lumber, boxcars for flour, loaves and furniture, and boxcars or covered hoppers (from 1930) for grain. A stop can couple any mix of the cars the train owns.
  • Cargo: tap a station or an industry to see what waits there. Tap the cash box for the ledger.
  • Finance: in the ledger, open Company to issue bonds, sell or buy back shares, trade your own shares and set a yearly dividend. Three month ends in a row with negative cash restructure the company.
